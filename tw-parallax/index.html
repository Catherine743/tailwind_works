ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Artificial Intelligence (AI) means

making computers and machines think and act like humans.

It helps machines to:

  • 1.Learn from experience
  • 2.Understand language
  • 3.Recognize faces
  • 4.Make decisions

For example: Google Assistant, ChatGPT, self-driving cars, and face unlock in phones all use AI.

Early idea (1950s)

In 1950, a scientist named Alan Turing asked:

"Can machines think?"

This question started the idea of AI.

In 1956, the term "Artificial Intelligence" was officially created.

Slow progress (1970s - 1990s)

  • AI grew slowly because computers were weak and costly.
  • It could only do simple tasks like games and calculations.
  • AI followed fixed rules and could not learn.
  • There was very little data available.
  • This period is called the AI Winter.

Big growth (2000s)

Computers became faster and the internet gave a lot of data.

AI started improving in:

  • Searching on Google
  • Voice recognition
  • Online shopping suggestions

AI today (2010s - now)

Modern AI can:

  • Talk like humans (ChatGPT)
  • Drive cars
  • Diagnose diseases
  • Create images, music, and videos
